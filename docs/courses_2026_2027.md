## Data conventions

-   **Department:** `GENERAL`, `BIS`, or `ACCOUNTING`.
-   **Semester:** `first` or `second`.
-   **Type:** `mandatory` or `elective`.
-   **Prerequisites:** course codes required before registration; `—`
    means none.
-   Shared courses can appear more than once when their program offering
    differs.
